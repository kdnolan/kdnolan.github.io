How to read these reports

Each report opens with a one-line verdict and a short version you can read in two minutes. Below that, one section per topic: the computers we watch, software updates, safe settings, viruses, file changes, logins, and how we look from the internet. Each section carries a traffic light and ends with a plain explanation of the numbers. The to-do list at the bottom is in priority order, and a glossary translates any term you don't recognise.

From the second report onwards, each page also says what changed since the week before, so improvements and new problems are easy to spot without reading both.

The traffic lights

Fine Normal activity, nothing to do.

Watch Not dangerous today, but worth a look or a small fix.

Act now A real weakness an attacker could use. Fix this month.
